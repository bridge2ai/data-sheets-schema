## Shared generation rules v1

Apply these before emitting any value, during reconciliation, and during final
source review. The declared sources and their qualifications govern; these
instructions are not factual evidence or a target amount of content.

- A team, consortium, contact or leadership listing does not establish a
  creator, maintainer or reviewer relationship. A passage must assign that
  responsibility for the declared dataset or release. Review the containing
  entity and the role represented by its slot, not only whether its name is
  present in a source.
- An ongoing operation is in_progress, not applied or completed. Preserve
  planned, possible and conditional statements; a capability is not evidence
  that it was used on this dataset.
- A date governs only the bullet, clause or explicitly stated scope it heads.
  Do not extend it to neighboring observations. A capture or snapshot date
  dates that capture, not necessarily the underlying observation.
- Carry source-supported lineage and derivation in was_derived_from,
  parent_datasets or a complete textual provenance statement. Complete prose
  lineage is valid; an empty structured slot is not proof of omission. A
  biological source, earlier release or related study is not automatically a
  parent dataset.
- Populate variable metadata only for names explicitly identified as variables
  or fields in actual data files or their data dictionary. Broad data-type
  rows, mathematical symbols and generic descriptions of columns are not
  thereby named data-file variables.
- Carry a software/tool version only when its source states its use for this
  dataset's declared release and activity. Keep processing and metadata
  packaging roles distinct. A citation, companion analysis, hosting badge,
  standard, license or dataset release number does not establish that use.
- For non-entity multivalued purposes, preprocessing_strategies,
  collection_mechanisms and subpopulations, emit one entry per source-enumerated
  item and per distinct method or purpose explicitly named in prose. Follow
  governing lists and headings and retain qualifications. Do not split one
  item solely at commas, slashes or conjunctions or merge distinct items.
- For role-bearing rosters, first identify the governing list that establishes
  the actual role and scope. Emit every supported member of that list or none,
  and identify an incomplete list in source_caveats. Never manufacture members
  to satisfy a count; membership in another list does not establish this role.

## Role relationship review v1

Review each complete role-bearing containing entity under its actual induced
schema owner and slot. A supported name alone does not support creator,
maintainer, reviewer or other responsibility for the declared dataset/release.
Assess the relationship and its scope against the governing source passage.
This applies to original audit rows and to every final report/source_review row,
including unchanged members and report_regate. Preserve the existing claim and
source-review grammar; do not relabel a contradiction as supported to finish.
Schema context identifies the role being asserted, not evidence that it is true.
