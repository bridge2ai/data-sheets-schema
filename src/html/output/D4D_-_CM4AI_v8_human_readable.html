Motivation

For what purpose was the dataset created?

  • Response
    To deliver machine-readable hierarchical maps of cell architecture as AI-Ready data, produced from multimodal interrogation of 100 chromatin modifiers and 100 metabolic enzymes involved in cancer, neuropsychiatric and cardiac disorders, in disease-relevant cell lines under perturbed and unperturbed conditions.
  • Response
    To produce ethical, AI-ready datasets of cell architecture, inferred from multimodal data collected for human cell lines, in order to enable transformative biomedical artificial intelligence research.
  • Response
    To map the spatiotemporal architecture of human cells and use these maps toward the grand challenge of interpretable genotype-phenotype learning, creating a library of large-scale maps of cellular structure and function across demographic and disease contexts.

Dataset
The June 2026 Data Release (Beta) of Cell Maps for Artificial Intelligence (CM4AI), the Functional Genomics Grand Challenge of the NIH Bridge2AI program. CM4AI generates multi-modal data — protein-protein interaction, spatial protein localization, and genetic perturbation — in MDA-MB-468 breast cancer cells (with or without paclitaxel or vorinostat) and in KOLF2.1J induced pluripotent stem cells (with or without differentiation). This release distributes ten ZIP archives through the University of Virginia Dataverse (LibraData) and comprises: perturb-seq data for MDA-MB-468 breast cancer cells with and without treatment and for undifferentiated (parental) KOLF2.1J iPSCs; SEC-MS data for MDA-MB-468 breast cancer cells with and without treatment, undifferentiated KOLF2.1J iPSCs, and iPSC-derived neuron progenitor cells, neurons and cardiomyocytes; AP-MS data for treated MDA-MB-468 breast cancer cells; and immunofluorescence images of MDA-MB-468 breast cancer cells with and without treatment. Corresponding raw mass spectrometry and sequence data are deposited in external repositories. The release is explicitly interim: computed cell maps are not included, and some component datasets remain under temporary pre-publication embargo.

  • Response
    In genomics and precision medicine, machine learning models are often "black boxes" that predict phenotypes from genotypes without revealing the mechanisms by which such translation occurs. This dataset is intended to supply the matched data and tools needed to build "visible" machine learning systems built directly on knowledge maps of cell and tissue architecture.
  • Response
    Existing biomedical datasets frequently lack the properties needed for reliable AI use. This project addresses that gap by defining and supplying AI-Ready data: FAIR data of known provenance, fully characterized and validated, with machine-readable provenance graphs of computations, data, software and models, explainable within stated limitations, and ethically obtained, licensed and distributed.
