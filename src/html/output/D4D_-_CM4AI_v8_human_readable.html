CM4AI June 2026 Data Release (Beta)
Cell Maps for Artificial Intelligence - June 2026 Data Release (Beta)
The June 2026 Data Release of Cell Maps for Artificial Intelligence (CM4AI), the Functional Genomics Grand Challenge in the NIH Bridge2AI program. CM4AI generates multi-modal data including protein-protein interaction (PPI), spatial localization, and genetic perturbation data in MDA-MB-468 breast cancer cells (with and without paclitaxel or vorinostat treatment) and induced pluripotent stem cells (with and without differentiation). This Beta release includes perturb-seq data for MDA-MB-468 breast cancer cells with and without treatment and undifferentiated (parental) KOLF2.1J iPSCs; SEC-MS data for MDA-MB-468 breast cancer cells with and without treatment, undifferentiated KOLF2.1J iPSCs, and iPSC-derived neuron progenitor cells (NPCs), neurons, and cardiomyocytes; AP-MS data for MDA-MB-468 breast cancer cells with treatment; and immunofluorescence images in MDA-MB-468 breast cancer cells with and without treatment. The overarching objective of CM4AI is to deliver machine-readable hierarchical maps of cell architecture as AI-ready data, produced from multimodal interrogation of chromatin modifiers, metabolic enzymes, and other proteins involved in cancer, neuropsychiatric, and cardiac disorders in disease-relevant cell lines under treated and untreated conditions.

  • Response
    In genomics and precision medicine, machine learning models are often "black boxes," predicting phenotypes from genotypes without understanding the mechanisms by which such translation occurs. This project addresses that deficiency by creating a library of large-scale maps of cellular structure and function across demographic and disease contexts.
  • Response
    Cell maps provide a foundation for downstream applications in human genomics, including interpretation of genetic variants and mutations, which existing black-box models do not support; CM4AI addresses this by producing AI-ready hierarchical maps of cell architecture from multimodal interrogation of disease-relevant cell lines.

CM4AI consists of three pillars - Data, People, and Ethics - organized into six modules: Data Acquisition, Tools, and Standards (Data); Skills and Workforce Development, and Teaming (People); and Ethics. The project defines AI-Ready biomedical data as fully characterized FAIR data of known provenance that can be ethically and reliably processed by AI applications, whose models and software are available and well-described for validation and re-use, and whose predictions may be fully explained and interpreted to the user as needed; the project's stated criteria are FAIR, Provenanced, Characterized, Explainable, and Ethical. The Standards Module's FAIRSCAPE framework assigns persistent, resolvable, globally unique identifiers using the ARK scheme, with provenance entailments expressed in the EVI Evidence Graph Ontology. The CM4AI Tools Module maintains the Multi-Scale Integrated Cell (MuSIC) pipeline, which integrates protein-protein interaction and immunofluorescence image data by node2vec and Human Protein Atlas deep learning embeddings, contrastive co-embedding, and multi-resolution community detection, and which annotates resulting assemblies by alignment to Gene Ontology and Reactome and by a large language model naming approach; this pipeline produces the computed cell maps that the June 2026 release states are not included in this release.

  • Response
