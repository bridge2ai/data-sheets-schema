CHoRUS
CHoRUS Dataset
The CHoRUS dataset is a multicenter, multimodal, high-resolution, controlled-access dataset of acute and critical care hospital admissions assembled by the Patient-Focused Collaborative Hospital Repository Uniting Standards (CHoRUS) for Equitable AI project, one of four data generation projects in the NIH-funded Bridge2AI consortium. Data are collected retrospectively from 14 contributing hospitals drawn from a network of 20 academic centers and are standardized to the OMOP Common Data Model, with imaging from PACS in DICOM, bedside telemetry in WFDB, EEG in EDF+ and Persyst, and clinical notes extracted and tokenized with the OHNLP toolkit. The current released dataset described on chorus4ai.org comprises 50,000 patient admissions from ICU, PICU and NICU, 1.6 billion rows of EHR OMOP data, 7,642 admissions with radiology data, and 23 Tb of waveform data; the anticipated final dataset is 100,000 patient admissions across 9 data modalities. All data types are controlled access. OMOP and telemetry data are worked with inside the Bridge2AI AI/ML for Clinical Care Collaborative Cloud enclave; clinical notes remain stored locally at contributing sites with only tokens available. The dataset is intended to support characterizing acute and critical care illness, predicting complications, and measuring treatment response, and the award abstract states that a holdout test set will be provisioned for external validation of models.
