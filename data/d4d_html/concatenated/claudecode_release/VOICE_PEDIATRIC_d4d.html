Bridge2AI-Voice Pediatric Dataset
The Bridge2AI-Voice Pediatric Dataset v1.1.0 contains derived audio features for 23,533 recordings collected from 300 participants aged 2-18. The release contains data considered low risk, including derivations such as spectrograms but not the original voice recordings. Detailed demographic, clinical, and validated questionnaire data are also made available. The dataset holds derived audio data features, phenotypic information acquired during data collection, and metadata information for the recordings.

Limited availability of pediatric voice datasets
The availability of large-scale pediatric datasets is limited; the challenges of data collection in pediatric populations (privacy issues, consent processes, the need for developmentally appropriate tasks) have contributed to the lack of publicly available pediatric data sets that enable machine learning for pediatric voice analysis.
Adult evidence does not generalize to children
Pediatric and adult voice and speech production are fundamentally different, so the evidence and normative data available for adults cannot be generalized to pediatric populations.
