## Findings left as-is

### `multispectral_light_intensity` not split into eleven variables

Finding 9 asked for one object per distinct variable. The particulate-matter and nitrogen-oxide channels were split because the sources name each fraction and each gas individually (PM1.0, PM2.5, PM4, PM10; NO, NO2). The sources describe the light measurements only as "Multi-spectral light intensity measurements (11)" without naming the eleven spectral channels. Splitting would have required inventing eleven variable names. The entry was kept as a single `array`-typed variable and a `notes` was added stating that the sources describe the eleven measurements collectively without naming the channels, and that this is why they are recorded as one array variable.

### Verified-correct content that the audit confirmed

The audit's summary confirmed that the nine `file_collections` sum to 356,334 files against a declared total of 356,343, the difference being the nine root CDS metadata files. Rather than leave that gap unexplained, `file_collections[9]` (`#root-metadata`) now carries `file_count: 9`, making the totals reconcile exactly. All split, sex, race/ethnicity and diabetes-status counts, all enum values, the bare `doi`, the UTC-offset `issued`, and all ROR and ORCID identifiers were confirmed correct and were not altered.
