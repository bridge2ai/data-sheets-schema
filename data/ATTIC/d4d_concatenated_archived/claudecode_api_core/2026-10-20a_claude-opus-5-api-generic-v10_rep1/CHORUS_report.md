# Reconciliation report — CHoRUS dataset (CHORUS)

## 1. What the audit found

The computed audit counts record **14 findings: 2 medium, 12 low** (no high).

Two findings concerned values already present in the original full record:

- **`/raw_data_sources/2/source_description` (medium)** — "Waveform EEG obtained from a hospital database." asserts a completed acquisition, while the September 2025 webinar slide that names the hospital database also states "EEG – extraction in process at this point".
- **`/intended_uses/1/usage_notes` (low)** — "trainees receive hands-on training …" is present tense, while the webinar states "Trainees **will** receive hands-on training on the Bridge2AI AI/ML for Clinical Care Network" and gives the Cohort 2 Program Start Date as November 17, 2025, after the September 09, 2025 webinar.

The remaining twelve findings were omission findings, carrying 19 declared omission candidates across the four bundled sources: the planned patient-level acquisition target; the planned ethics focus groups, contextual-factor commitment and collaboration structure; the planned data-category enumeration including social determinants of health; the education/AI-literacy aim; three statements about the training programme's use of the data; the eligible-organizations panel; the NIH website disclaimer; the cloud-enclave tooling statement and the site's programme-manager contact; the 20-centers/14-acquisition distinction and the privacy/bias/SDoH commitment; the unified-standards harmonization statement; the GoogleScript status automation; and three named GitHub resources. The audit raised no rejected relationship: no `remove_relationship` action was declared, so no structural removal was performed.
