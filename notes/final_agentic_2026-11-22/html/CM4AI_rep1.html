Description
The overarching mission of CM4AI is to produce ethical, AI-ready datasets of cell architecture, inferred from multimodal data collected for human cell lines, to enable transformative biomedical AI research.
CM4AI's objective is to deliver machine-readable hierarchical maps of cell architecture as AI-ready data produced from multimodal interrogation of 100 chromatin modifiers and 100 metabolic enzymes involved in cancer, neuropsychiatric, and cardiac disorders.
The CM4AI data generation project seeks to map the spatiotemporal architecture of human cells and use these maps toward the grand challenge of interpretable genotype-phenotype learning.

Cell Maps For AI (CM4AI)
Cell Maps For AI: Mapping Cellular Structure and Function
https://cm4ai.org/
CM4AI (Cell Maps for Artificial Intelligence) is the Functional Genomics data generation project of the NIH Bridge2AI program. This record describes the CM4AI dataset as an ongoing series of data releases deposited in the University of Virginia Dataverse; the individual releases are listed under resources. The project states that it will deliver machine-readable hierarchical maps of cell architecture as AI-ready data, together with quarterly data releases of map-input data streams. CM4AI generates multi-modal data, including protein-protein interaction (PPI), spatial localization and genetic perturbation data, in MDA-MB-468 breast cancer cells (with or without paclitaxel or vorinostat treatment) and in induced pluripotent stem cells (with or without differentiation), using three complementary mapping approaches: proteomic mass spectrometry, cellular imaging, and genetic perturbation via CRISPR/Cas9. CM4AI is a collaboration of UCSD, UCSF, Stanford, UVA, Yale, UT Austin, UA Birmingham, Simon Fraser University, and the Hastings Center, as part of the NIH Bridge2AI program. The project website's undated "Data Insights" panel lists 1,374 protein interactions, 53,788 immunofluorescent images, 7,023 total proteins investigated, 11,739 genes targeted and a data volume of 21.4 TB.

Per the May 2024 preprint, input data streams are integrated via the multi-scale integrated cell (MuSIC) software pipeline employing deep learning models and community detection algorithms to produce cell maps.
Multi-Scale Integrated Cell (MuSIC) pipeline
Software comprising the Tools data integration pipeline is available under the BSD-3 open source license in GitHub (alpha-level tools) or in the Zenodo long-term archive (production-ready tools).
Downloaded PPI networks are processed using the node2vec deep learning model; IF images are processed using a Human Protein Atlas deep learning model; the PPI and image embeddings are integrated to obtain a co-embedding for each protein using contrastive deep learning; community detection on the co-embedding produces a hierarchy of protein communities that is output as the final cell map.
The June 2026 release states that computed cell maps are not included in it, so this pipeline describes how the project's cell maps are produced rather than a step applied to the files in the current release.
FAIRSCAPE-CLI creates an RO-Crate package with the datasets, metadata, and software, which the FAIRSCAPE server registers and assigns persistent identifiers.
FAIRSCAPE RO-Crate packaging
The May 2024 preprint states that the PID system currently in use is the ARK scheme, with DOIs a future feature as supplementary PIDs for final-state publishable work.
