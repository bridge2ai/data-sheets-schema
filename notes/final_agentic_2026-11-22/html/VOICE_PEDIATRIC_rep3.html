Bridge2AI Voice consortium
Bridge2AI-Voice Pediatric Dataset v1.1.0 contains derived audio features for 23,533 recordings collected from 300 participants aged 2-18 at the Hospital for Sick Children. The release contains data considered low risk, including derivations such as spectrograms, but not the original voice recordings. Detailed demographic, clinical, and validated questionnaire data are also made available. The release is organized into derived audio features (features), phenotypic information acquired during data collection (phenotype), and per-recording metadata (metadata). Metrics related to audio quality derived from the recordings are provided in an audio_quality_metrics.tsv file.

  • Name
    Eligibility criteria at the Hospital for Sick Children
    Description
    Patients and healthy volunteers at the Hospital for Sick Children were considered for enrollment; pediatric participants are recruited strictly from SickKids. Inclusion criteria were 2 to 18 years of age and English proficiency. Exclusion criteria included participants over 18 years of age and individuals who were non-verbal. Non-patients, recruited through research postings, were evaluated for eligibility against the same criteria.
DescriptionName
Once consented, patients were assigned a unique study identification number and a standardized age-appropriate protocol was adopted, including demographic information, voice and speech related questionnaires, and questionnaires about medical history.
Standardized age-appropriate collection protocol
All data was collected through customized software (reproschema-ui) on tablets with the Bridge2AI-Voice pediatric protocol; voice, breathing, cough and speech data are recorded with reproschema-ui for pediatrics.
reproschema-ui on tablets
A headset was used to record most participants; the remaining recordings used the built-in tablet microphone due to low tolerance of wearing headphones or existing complex medical conditions.
Headset or built-in tablet microphone
The project documentation's table of acoustic tasks lists tasks for the pediatric protocol, including ABC's (recalling the alphabet), Ready For School, Favorite Show, Long Sounds (sustained /ee/ and /ah/ sounds), Silly Sounds (/PUH/ /TUH/ /KUH/) and Repeat Sentences (sentence recall).
Pediatric acoustic tasks
