Description
To produce ethical, AI-ready datasets of cell architecture, inferred from multimodal data collected for human cell lines, to enable transformative biomedical AI research.
To deliver machine-readable hierarchical maps of cell architecture as AI-ready data produced from multimodal interrogation of 100 chromatin modifiers and 100 metabolic enzymes involved in cancer, neuropsychiatric, and cardiac disorders in disease-relevant cell lines under perturbed and unperturbed conditions (stated as the project's objective in the 2024 preprint).
To map the spatiotemporal architecture of human cells and use these maps toward the grand challenge of interpretable genotype-phenotype learning.

CM4AI
Cell Maps for Artificial Intelligence
https://cm4ai.org/
CM4AI (Cell Maps for Artificial Intelligence) is the Functional Genomics Grand Challenge data generation project in the NIH Bridge2AI program. The project states that it will deliver machine-readable hierarchical maps of cell architecture as AI-ready data, together with quarterly data releases of map-input data streams, using three complementary mapping approaches: proteomic mass spectrometry, cellular imaging, and genetic perturbation via CRISPR/Cas9. CM4AI is generating multi-modal data including protein-protein interaction (PPI), spatial localization, and genetic perturbation data in MDA-MB-468 breast cancer cells (with or without paclitaxel or vorinostat) and induced pluripotent stem cells (with or without differentiation). The data releases are deposited in the University of Virginia Dataverse; this record describes the release series, with each release captured in the bundle listed under resources and the June 2026 Data Release (Beta), doi:10.18130/V3/HIGT4C, as the current release. CM4AI is a collaboration of UCSD, UCSF, Stanford, UVA, Yale, UT Austin, UA Birmingham, Simon Fraser University, and the Hastings Center. The project website reports 1,374 protein interactions, 53,788 immunofluorescent images, 7,023 proteins investigated, 11,739 genes targeted and a data volume of 21.4 TB.

DescriptionNameSource Caveats
The 2024 preprint describes the pipeline by which CM4AI input data streams are integrated into cell maps: protein-protein interaction networks are embedded with the node2vec model and immunofluorescence images with a Human Protein Atlas deep learning model; the two embeddings are integrated into a co-embedding for each protein by contrastive deep learning; community detection on all-by-all protein similarities in the co-embedding produces a hierarchy of protein communities output as the cell map; and cell maps are annotated by alignment to Gene Ontology and Reactome and by a large language model naming approach.
Multi-Scale Integrated Cell (MuSIC) pipeline
The June 2026 release states that computed cell maps are not included in that release, so this pipeline describes how CM4AI cell maps are produced, not processing applied to the released input data files.
