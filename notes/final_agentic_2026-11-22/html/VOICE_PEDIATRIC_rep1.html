Bridge2AI-Voice Pediatric Dataset version 1.1.0, published on PhysioNet under credentialed access, contains derived audio features for 23,533 recordings collected from 300 participants aged 2-18. The release contains data its publishers consider low risk, including derivations such as spectrograms, but not the original voice recordings; detailed demographic, clinical and validated questionnaire data are also made available. The data are organized as derived audio features (features folder), phenotypic information acquired during data collection (phenotype folder) and per-recording metadata (metadata folder). Raw pediatric audio is not part of the PhysioNet project and is distributed separately through Synapse under a more involved access process.

NameResponse
Lack of open pediatric voice datasets for machine learning
Privacy issues, consent processes and the need for developmentally appropriate tasks in pediatric data collection have contributed to a lack of publicly available pediatric datasets that enable machine learning for pediatric voice analysis.
Sparse pediatric voice and speech analysis literature
The parent Bridge2AI Voice Data Acquisition protocol states that the literature on pediatric voice and speech analysis is sparser, partly due to ethical concerns and challenges in data acquisition for this cohort, and that a voice database of a normal cohort of different age groups will be key to help machine learning models diagnose age-specific speech delays and disorders.
  • Name
    Eligibility and recruitment
    Strategies
    Patients and healthy volunteers at the Hospital for Sick Children (Toronto, Ontario, Canada) were considered for enrollment; pediatric participants are recruited strictly from SickKids. Inclusion criteria were 2 to 18 years of age and English proficiency; exclusion criteria were age over 18 and being non-verbal. Non-patients, recruited through research postings, were evaluated against the same inclusion and exclusion criteria.
  • Name
    reproschema-ui on tablets
    Mechanism Details
    All data were collected through customized software (reproschema-ui) on tablets, with the Bridge2AI-Voice pediatric protocol. A headset was used to record most participants; the remaining recordings used the built-in tablet microphone because of low tolerance of wearing headphones or existing complex medical conditions. Voice, breathing, cough and speech data are recorded with reproschema-ui for pediatrics. All participants completed the recording and demographic data collection in one session; for participants without adequate comprehension and familiarity with their past medical history, parents or decision-makers completed the survey during the recording on a separate tablet.
  • Name
    Standardized age-appropriate protocol
    Acquisition Details
    Once consented, participants were assigned a unique study identification number and a standardized age-appropriate protocol was adopted. The protocol included the collection of demographic information, voice and speech related questionnaires, and questionnaires inquiring about medical history.

Special Protections
  • The parent Bridge2AI Voice Data Acquisition protocol states that pediatric patients will be enrolled only through the pediatric sites and not at USF, and that data collection for pediatric cohorts will only be performed at the pediatric participating institutions.
  • The parent protocol states that Checklist HRP-416 for children under 21 has been completed and is appended to the protocol.
Guardian Consent
  • The parent protocol states that when children participate, parents or guardians will provide consent, and that parental permission will be obtained from one or both parents because the research is minimal risk.
  • The parent protocol excludes pediatric participants who do not receive consent from a parent or guardian to voice and clinical de-identified data being uploaded to an open-sourced database.
Assent Procedures
The parent protocol states that children will provide verbal assent where appropriate, and that young children who cannot reasonably be asked to assent because of age and development (typically children under seven years of age) will not be asked to do so.

NamePreprocessing DetailsUsed Software
Export and conversion to tab-delimited values
Data were exported and converted to tab-delimited values using an open-source library developed by the study team.
Audio feature derivation and phenotype merging
Raw audio waveforms were preprocessed into Parquet feature files (torchaudio spectrograms, Mel spectrograms, MFCCs and pitch; SPARC-based EMA, loudness, periodicity and pitch; phonetic posteriorgrams), static features were derived with openSMILE, Praat, parselmouth and torchaudio, and the source data were merged into the phenotype files. The code used for this has been made open source in the b2aiprep library.
