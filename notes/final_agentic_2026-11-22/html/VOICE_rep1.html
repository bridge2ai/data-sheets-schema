Motivation

For what purpose was the dataset created?

DescriptionNameSource Caveats
Build a large multi-institutional database of human voices, speech and respiratory sounds that is ethically sourced, diverse, and linked to multimodal health biomarkers to fuel voice AI research; the project seeks to create an ethically sourced flagship dataset to enable future research in artificial intelligence and support insights into the use of voice as a biomarker of health, and to build predictive models to assist in screening, diagnosis, and treatment of a broad range of diseases.
Ethically sourced, diverse voice database linked to health biomarkers
Stated enrollment targets differ by source: the IRB protocol gives a sample size of 30 000 participants and the white paper names a primary deliverable of a publicly available database of 30 000 human voices, while the project documentation states that the project seeks to create a flagship dataset of 10,000 voices linked to health information.
Target database size
Targets, not achieved counts. The IRB protocol and the project documentation share manifest tier 2 and disagree (30 000 versus 10,000); the white paper (tier 3) agrees with the protocol. Their dates and scopes differ and the ranking cannot decide between the equal-tier sources, so both figures are recorded.
Enable the development, benchmarking, or validation of clinically applicable machine-learning models for diagnosing a wide range of health conditions using voice data.
Enable clinically applicable machine-learning models

Audio standardization
Features were extracted from the raw audio to limit re-identification, and only the extracted features are released.
Feature extraction
Spectrograms computed with the short-time FFT (25 ms window, 10 ms hop, 400-point FFT) and downsampled by a factor of two in time; 60 MFCCs extracted from the spectrograms; Mel spectrograms (60 Mels); articulatory features from the Speech Articulatory Coding (sparc) package, including electromagnetic articulography (EMA) estimates plus loudness, periodicity and pitch; acoustic features from OpenSMILE (eGeMAPS features per audio file); phonetic and prosodic features from Parselmouth and Praat; and phonetic posteriorgrams (PPGs) from the ppgs package; torchaudio-based pitch contours.

HIPAA Safe Harbor identifiers were removed; identifiable information under HIPAA and the Common Rule, as well as data considered sensitive, has been removed.
Identifiers Removed
All direct identifiers (such as name, civic address and social security numbers); state and province (country of data collection was retained); all raw voice data.
Deidentification Details
Indirect identifiers were removed where they created a significant risk of re-identification. Raw audio transcripts were reviewed, and audio recordings that contained potentially identifying information or external voices were removed. Spectrograms and similar features were excluded if the audio contained free speech, while static and other features that do not encode the sensitive information were retained. Spectrograms, Mel spectrograms, MFCCs, PPGs and EMAs for sensitive records and audio checks have been removed from v3.1.
