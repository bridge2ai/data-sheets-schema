  • CDS
Version 3.0.0 of the AI-READI dataset, published on the FAIRhub platform as "Flagship Dataset of Type 2 Diabetes from the AI-READI Project". The AI-READI project seeks to create and share a flagship ethically sourced dataset of type 2 diabetes. The dataset consists of data collected from individuals with and without type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) and harmonized across 3 data collection sites; its composition was designed for future AI/machine learning studies, with recruitment sampling aimed at an approximately equal distribution of participants across sex, race and diabetes severity, and a data acquisition protocol spanning multiple domains (survey data, physical measurements, clinical data, imaging data, wearable device data). This version contains data from 2280 participants collected between July 19, 2023 and May 1, 2025. The modalities encompass tabular data, imaging data and physiological signal/waveform data; there is no unstructured text data. The dataset contains 356,343 files and is around 3.82 TB in size, organized following the Clinical Dataset Structure (CDS) v0.1.1, with one directory per datatype at the root level, then one folder per modality, per device and per participant.

FalseTriple-balanced recruitment design
Rather than targeting the demographic distribution of the US population, the study is triple-balanced by recruiting in equal proportions four race/ethnic groups (Asian, Black, White, Hispanic), four categories of T2DM status (no diabetes; pre-diabetes/lifestyle-controlled; oral or non-insulin-injectable medication controlled; insulin controlled) and biological males and females, with recruitment sampling aimed at approximately equal distribution across diabetes severity. The investigators aim to recruit approximately 1000 patients into each of the four groups.
The design intentionally balances groups rather than mirroring the demographic distribution of the US population.
Eligibility criteria
Inclusion: able to provide consent, 40 years or older, persons with or without type 2 diabetes, able to speak and read English. Exclusion: pregnancy, gestational diabetes, type 1 diabetes; the study description and IRB protocol also exclude adults older than 85.
EHR-based recruitment in waves
The study base is patients aged 40 or older with a medical encounter at a site health system (UAB, UCSD, UW). Recruitment pools are identified by screening electronic health records for diabetes and prediabetes ICD-10 codes, and participants are recruited in waves; as recruitment progresses the composition of participants is monitored for diversity and inclusion and adjusted by under- and oversampling groups as needed. The IRB protocol describes UAB selecting a stratified sample of potential participants based on the sampling scheme.

Version Details
The dataset is released as static versions. Version 1.0.0 (pilot) contained data from 204 participants; version 2.0.0 added data from 863 participants for a total of 1067; version 3.0.0 contains a total of 2280 participants (the README's change table lists 1213 participants as year 3 data included in v3.0.0). The publicly accessible dataset from pilot data collection was made available in May 2024 and data collected through July 31, 2024 in November 2024. In version 3.0.0 the Snellen visual acuity variables were dropped, so only logMAR visual acuity measurements are available. The version 2.0.0 documentation states that version is no longer accessible.
Source Caveats
The v3.0.0 documentation's About page says v3.0 "contains data from the participants of the pilot study phase", which disagrees with the FAIRhub dataset metadata and healthsheet (2280 participants collected July 19, 2023 to May 1, 2025). The FAIRhub structured metadata (source tier 1) is preferred over the documentation page (tier 2); the documentation sentence appears to be carried over from an earlier version's page.
