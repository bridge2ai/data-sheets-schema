- A slot whose declared range is an enumeration is populated only from a
  passage that states the category, in the source's own words or a plain
  restatement of them — never from what a value's name, unit or position
  suggests. A variable's `data_type` and a collection's `collection_type`
  are claims about how a thing is classified, and a passage that names the
  thing without classifying it supports the name and not the class. Two
  enumeration slots, `confidentiality_level` and `hipaa_compliant`, are also
  populated from a passage that states the facts below: such a passage is a
  plain restatement of the value those facts support, and it is the slot's
  coverage receipt. `confidentiality_level` (ConfidentialityLevelEnum)
  records who can obtain this dataset's data, and how. A passage stating
  that the data are publicly available to download, that anyone can download
  them, or that downloading them needs no account, registration, approval or
  signed agreement supports `unrestricted`, unless a passage states a
  condition below for the same data. A public licence such as Creative
  Commons, a download page asking the user to confirm that licence, a
  request to cite, or a form asking who is downloading is not such a
  condition. A passage stating that access requires registration or an
  account, verified identity or credentials, approval of the request,
  sign-off by the requester's institution, or a data use agreement or
  dataset-specific licence that the requester or their institution signs or
  accepts supports `restricted`. A passage stating that each request is
  reviewed before access is granted, by a data access committee or office or
  by an institutional review board or ethics committee approving the
  requester's own use, supports `confidential`; a study's own ethics or IRB
  approval of collecting its data is not such review. A route that requires
  such review is `confidential` whatever else it requires; one that requires
  only conditions supporting `restricted` is `restricted`. A gated tier's
  name, such as "registered access", "controlled access" or "credentialed
  access", states no conditions: it is recorded as prose and is never the
  receipt, and where no passage states the conditions of the tier the slot
  would record, the slot stays empty. Where the documents describe several
  access tiers for this dataset, the slot records the least restrictive tier
  through which any of this dataset's own data can be obtained; a tier
  offering only metadata, documentation, code, a sample or a synthetic copy
  does not count. Each tier, with its conditions and the data it covers, is
  described in `data_governance.access_review_process`. A statement of how
  sensitive the data are describes the data, not how access works, and does
  not decide `confidentiality_level`. In `hipaa_compliant`
  (ComplianceStatusEnum), a passage stating that the dataset holds no data
  about human participants — for example, only cell lines, model organisms
  or simulated data — supports `not_applicable`. A passage stating that the
  data were de-identified under HIPAA, whether it names HIPAA or its Safe
  Harbor or Expert Determination method, supports `compliant`; a passage
  stating only that the data contain no protected health information, or
  that they were de-identified without naming HIPAA or either method,
  supports neither value. No other enumeration slot is read this way:
  `data_type`, `collection_type` and every other enumeration slot remain
  under the rest of this rule. A label from another classification scheme —
  an institution's data-security level such as "Level 3", an HL7
  confidentiality code such as "HL7:2N" — is recorded as prose where
  relevant and is never mapped to an enumeration value or used as the
  receipt for one. Where no passage states the category the slot stays empty
  — an empty enumeration slot is a gap the reader can see, a guessed one an
  error the reader cannot — except where the schema requires the slot, in
  which case the entry itself is what the evidence must support: a related
  dataset is recorded only where a passage states what the relation is. A
  file's `format`, `file_type`, `media_type` and `encoding`, and a file's or
  collection's `compression`, are read from the file the bundle names — its
  name and extension are the passage for them — and this rule does not reach
  those.
