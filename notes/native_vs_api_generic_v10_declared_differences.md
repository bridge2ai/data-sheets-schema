## Acceptance and interpretation boundary

The U5 preparation may exercise selection, render, cold/warm reuse and scanner checks without the installed protected bundle. Its report must name each head and every blocked gate. Final acceptance requires the accepted U1+U3 source, A6/C7/D10 integration, released D108-compliant schema, approved four-packet installation, genuine complete-path tests, independent adversarial review, exact-head full CI and merged-byte verification. An uncompleted scan or a known protected-hook refusal is not a pass. This document remains DRAFT until that evidence is reconciled.

The comparison can describe outcomes of the **two registered complete packages**, including actual failures, evidence coverage, timing and observed accounting within each route's limits. It cannot isolate the effect of agentic execution alone, turn workers into replicates, establish superiority from CHORUS n=1, infer scientific support from copied source text, or equate different receipt denominators. Exact text preservation does not justify `exactMatch`/`none`; source coverage cannot improve merely by retiring rows. Human scientific review, applicability/recall annotations and rating authorization remain distinct gates.

No native executable, permission probe, account access, provider call, launch or rating is performed in preparing this declaration. The companion [draft operator guide](native_operator_guide_DRAFT.md) is a non-executable checklist and records the remaining N1 choices.
