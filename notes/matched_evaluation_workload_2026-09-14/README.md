variant. The 80 canary-cell ratings are included in the 256 total, not added

these canaries. Their absence from the 256 count does not remove them from
the plan. Historical adjudications remain separate from new scores.

## Budget interpretation

The inventory sums proposed whole-attempt ceilings to show potential
exposure: $180 for the 32 generations (30 × $5 plus two **unapproved** Kids
First × $15), and $1,280 for 256 rubric ratings at $5 each. The resulting
**$1,460 is not a forecast, reservation or spending authorization**. Actual
charges may be much lower, and the shared additional $200 ledger remains
binding. The two rejected canaries' estimated $6.322086 also remains charged
to that allocation. No exception or new condition resets the budget.

Measure accepted generation and evaluator canaries, retain all requests and
usage, then prepare the empirical full-workload cost projection and decision
before production expansion. Do not fund extra jobs by silently dropping a
runtime, dataset, variant, rubric, evaluation style or repeat. Do not treat
unpriced/interrupted historical attempts as zero-cost observations.

## Required launch registration

Each evaluator attempt still needs exact accepted original bytes, a trusted
applicability context, full instrument identity, runtime/model, request or
instructions, and exclusive output paths. Direct API quality scoring uses
the reviewed streaming support through the shared CBORG admission controller.
Both field and semantic agents require the pinned preamble and verified
check-echo. Register repeat panels before observing their scores; retain all
attempts, exclusions, quoted identities and original verdicts. Separate
generation-replicate variation from evaluator repeatability in the manuscript.

Validation checked all distinct record/rating identities and references,
paired source hashes and profiles, variant coverage, both rubrics, repeat
counts, the non-duplicated canary subtotal and cap arithmetic. Re-running the
enumerator reproduces the exact JSON bytes. This checks the proposal's
accounting; it does not validate or approve any future model output.
