## Resolution

- #1415: receipt joins, report checking, review packs and backfills use an
  explicit live run identity or the portable record being checked. A stale
  neighboring index cannot choose another run's phase evidence. Portable
  snapshots retain logical phase, generation and billed-response identities
  beside their paths and SHA256 values. Missing indexes recover only those
  attested bytes; missing or ambiguous modern phase evidence is unusable.
  Bytes are read once, verified, parsed and hashed from that same read.
  Recovery retains the complete snapshot history in its attested order.
- #1416: resume checks surviving current and readable archived indexes before
  the completed-run shortcut. An unrecorded generation or delivered usage ID
  requires its missing accounting evidence, even when an older completed
  record and all its artifact hashes still verify. Explicitly recorded prior
  generations remain preserved; opaque old indexes kept by an explicit
  restart are not treated as readable accounting evidence.
- #1417: strict chunk checks fail if an explicit bundle or a manifest-selected
  project's bundle is missing. Checks remain read-only.

The round-5 CI result was 3,831 passed, 2 failed, 4 skipped. Both failures were
outdated fixtures: the immutable phase inventory included the new mutable
index in its expected directory listing, and an artifact-corruption test had
no surviving phase-ownership evidence. Fixtures now distinguish recovery

## Validation and measurement boundary

The regression suite covers portable recovery with and without journals,
foreign and missing indexes, unregistered newer files, a crash immediately
after a paid full response, strict missing inputs, an atomic-read race,
ambiguous or foreign ownership, changed live inputs, a newer attempt hidden
behind a restored old index, and complete ordered history recovery. All
provider calls in these tests are fake; network access is blocked.

This changes recovery and checking behavior, not a scoring instrument or a
measured generation. No production generation, rating, source download or
prior evidence rewrite is authorized by this patch. The 56 existing ratings
and the historical v9 canary retain their original measured inputs. The
remaining evaluation, profiles and packaging stages follow the plan.
