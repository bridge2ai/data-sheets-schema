## D62 and F1/F2

The registration records the policy under
`bounded_carry.contract.sizing_calibration`: request bytes **2.48 bytes/token**
in every stage, future-response bytes **1.75 bytes/token**, measured escaping
**1.25**, and the gated/advisory scenario names. The code identity binds these
fields. The sizing format is `bc1_input_sizing_v3`; old sidecars cannot silently
inherit the changed policy.

For each integration stage, the exact empty-future production wire is its request
component. The ASCII-cap wire minus that floor is its future-response component.
The measured-cap scenario renders those same hypothetical ASCII inputs, records
their exact wire hash/size, and separately records the planning size after the
1.25 expansion. It does not claim that this arithmetic is a measured wire or
provider token count. Workers and omission have no future-response component.

```
planning_future_bytes = ceil(future_wire_bytes * 1.25)  # measured-cap scenario
input_tokens = ceil(request_bytes / 2.48 + planning_future_bytes / 1.75)
```

The floor and measured caps must fit both per-call room and aggregate allowance.
The escaped bound remains in the report, with truthful over-room flags; it does
not reject registration, even if its wire exceeds the byte ceiling. Real runtime
rendering/admission retains its limits. The library and CLI still prepare and
check before writing any output. The CHORUS legacy profile remains at 4M; A21
explicitly overrides the BC1 allowance to **4,500,000**.
