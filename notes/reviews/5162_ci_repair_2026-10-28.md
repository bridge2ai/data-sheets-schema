## Causes and changes

1. The specificity scanner refused the computed method receiver in
   `_omission_wire_prepared(packet, prepared).request()` as a projected receiving
   object. Naming the helper result alone still failed as a copied receiver alias.
   The final code explicitly constructs the frozen `audit_omissions.Prepared`
   value from the exact existing `payload_json` string before calling `request()`.
   This establishes a statically known receiver without reserializing payload
   bytes or changing selectors, validation, response binding, or scanner rules.
2. The two selected-tree fixture builders copied protocol assets, configuration,
   vocabulary and schemas, but not the executable dependencies A14's descriptor
   now reads. They now copy `shared_generation.REQUEST_CODE` from this checkout.
   Existing authority/refusal assertions remain; an additional missing-code case
   proves that an absent selected-tree request dependency cannot fall back to
   the caller's checkout.
3. The three source-heading failures were **not a pinned-set mismatch**. The old
   test expected shared-v1's descriptor to remain invariant after a dependency
   changed. A14 deliberately includes request-code hashes in both versions.
   The test now requires exactly the changed file's hash to move, with all other
   shared-v1 descriptor fields identical. The shared-v2 stale-registration refusal
   is retained. The three dependencies are `profile_identity.py`,
   `cache_dependencies.py`, and `historical_digest.py`. They were already members
   of `ROUTING_CODE`; A14 added `REQUEST_CODE = (*ROUTING_CODE, corpus.py,
   shared_write_footprint.py)` and its descriptor binding. This repair changes
   neither declared closure nor any pinned set.
