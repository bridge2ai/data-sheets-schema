## Resolution

Surviving reasoning and snapshot evidence is checked against recovered usage
before every further provider call and immediately before publication. This
covers partial resumes as well as completed returns. Ordinary paid phase
snapshots now carry their call IDs, so losing both a journal row and the
reasoning log still cannot silently erase the billed phase. Overlapping
portable and journal rows must agree on their counters, not just their IDs.

An explicit fresh generation archives the prior reasoning journal as opaque
bytes, including malformed bytes. A durable archive plan precedes the atomic
rename. A resume completes that same plan after an interruption before the
rename or before the final ledger write; the source/archive hash must match
and the old bytes are retained. Only the current journal is parsed for the
new generation. Active corruption remains a refusal.

Historical backfill candidates use replay-only restoration, including
recorded source/chunk paths, renderer versions and date substitutions. A
pre-field record tries explicit historical manifest conventions, never a
live registry lookup. Only an exact match to the original complete request
hash permits writing a recovered spec. New live specs still validate their
selected manifest. The header formatter is shared to preserve exact bytes.
