default can replace a historical observation, selection, or explicit absence.

then published by an exclusive hard link. If another writer creates the
destination in the meantime, backfill keeps it. An interrupted write leaves
no partial canonical record. Dry-run reports missing and preserved records
separately and publishes nothing.
