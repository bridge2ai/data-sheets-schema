    Description
    Each instance consists of all of the data available for an individual participating in the study. Multiple modalities of data are collected for each participant, including survey data, clinical data, retinal imaging data, environmental sensor data, continuous glucose monitor data, and wearable activity monitor data. These encompass tabular data, imaging data, and physiological signal/waveform data. There is no unstructured text data included in this dataset.
  1. Name
    Race/ethnicity, sex and diabetes status groups in the suggested split
    Identification
    Diabetes status groups are defined as: No DM, participants who do not have Type 1 or Type 2 Diabetes; Lifestyle, participants with pre-Type 2 Diabetes and those with Type 2 Diabetes whose blood sugar is controlled by lifestyle adjustments; Oral, participants with Type 2 Diabetes whose blood sugar is controlled by oral or injectable medications other than insulin; Insulin, participants with Type 2 Diabetes whose blood sugar is controlled by insulin.
    Distribution
    Totals in the README suggested-split summary (2280 participants): race/ethnicity Hispanic 380, Asian 545, Black 519, White 836; sex Male 951, Female 1329; diabetes status No DM 776, Lifestyle 560, Oral 686, Insulin 258; mean age 60.8 ± 11.3 years.
    Source Caveats
    The healthsheet answers "No" to whether the dataset identifies any demographic sub-populations, and states that sex, race, and ethnicity data are not being released with the public version of the dataset; the counts above are the summary table the README publishes for the suggested split.
  • Privacy Techniques
    The environmental sensor and the Garmin fitness tracker were specifically chosen with participant privacy as a primary concern. These devices do not capture video or audio, are not equipped with GPS location monitoring and are not synced with participant-owned devices.

doi:10.60775/fairhub.3
AI-READI dataset
Flagship Dataset of Type 2 Diabetes from the AI-READI Project
The AI-READI dataset consists of data collected from individuals with and without Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM) and harmonized across 3 data collection sites. This version contains data from 2280 participants that was collected between July 19, 2023 and May 01, 2025. Data from multiple modalities are included: survey data, clinical data, retinal imaging data, environmental sensor data, continuous glucose monitor data, and wearable activity monitor data. The data in this dataset contain no protected health information (PHI); information related to the sex and race/ethnicity of the participants as well as medication used has also been removed. The dataset contains 356,343 files and is around 3.82 TB in size.

  1. Name
    Participant recruitment
    Random Sampling
    False
    Representative Sample
    False
    Why Not Representative
    Building balanced data sets is critical for the development of unbiased machine learning models, so rather than targeting the demographic distribution of the US population, the study is triple-balanced by recruiting in equal proportions four race/ethnic groups (Asian, Black, White, Hispanic), four categories of T2DM (no diabetes, pre-diabetes or lifestyle-controlled diabetes, diabetes treated with oral or non-insulin injectable medications, and insulin-controlled diabetes) and biological males and females.
    Source Data
    The study base is all patients aged 40 years or older who had a medical encounter within each health system site (UAB, UCSD, UW) between 2020 and 2025; recruitment pools were identified by screening electronic health records for diabetes and prediabetes ICD-10 codes.
    Strategies
    Non-probability sample. Participants are recruited in waves; the composition of participants is monitored and will be adjusted by under- and oversampling groups as needed. Recruitment sampling procedures were aimed at achieving approximately equal distribution of participants across diabetes severity.
    Description
    Eligibility criteria: able to provide consent, 40 years old or older, persons with or without type 2 diabetes, must speak and read English; excluded are adults older than 85 years of age, pregnancy, gestational diabetes and Type 1 diabetes. The healthsheet states that the dataset contains all possible instances and that no data were excluded from the dataset at the time of preprocessing.

    The current version of the dataset comes with recommended data splits. The project team prepared data splits into proportions (70%/15%/15%) that can be used for subsequent training/validation/testing, where the validation and test sets are balanced as well as possible for sex, race/ethnicity and diabetes status. The README summary gives 1576 participants in Train, 352 in Val and 352 in Test, for a Total of 2280.
    Description
    The suggested split for training, validating, and testing AI/ML models is included in the participants.tsv file. It was prepared because sex, race, and ethnicity data are not being released with the public version of the dataset.

Mechanism DetailsName
REDCap is used for collecting patient-reported data from questionnaires and the following clinical data from the visit: medications assessment, vitals, visual acuity, contrast sensitivity, monofilament test results and CBC test results.
REDCap questionnaires and clinical data entry
A 12-lead ECG is performed (Philips Pagewriter TC30 Cardiograph) while the participant sits in a reclining chair or lies supine.
12-lead electrocardiogram
A general cognitive screener is carried out using the Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA), administered electronically on an iPad using the MoCA Duo Application.
Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA)
The monofilament test is done using a standard 10g monofilament applying pressure to different points on the plantar surface of the feet.
Monofilament testing for peripheral neuropathy
Photopic letter visual acuity is measured with the Electronic Visual Acuity tester (M&S Technology); mesopic acuity is measured by the participant viewing the display through a 2.0 neutral density (ND) filter.
Visual acuity testing
Photopic letter contrast sensitivity is measured with the Mars chart (Mars Perceptrix); mesopic contrast sensitivity is assessed by viewing through the 2.0 ND filter.
Contrast sensitivity testing
Autorefraction data for each eye are obtained using the autorefractor's standard protocol (Topcon KR 800).
Autorefraction
Blood (non-fasting) and urine samples are collected from participants during the visit. Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments-certified laboratories local to each data collection site perform complete blood count (CBC) testing on fresh whole blood samples; all other lab tests are performed by the University of Washington Nutrition and Obesity Research Center (NORC) testing facility using stored, frozen samples.
Blood and urine collection for laboratory testing
The Optomed Aurora IQ is a handheld fundus camera that can take non-mydriatic images of the ocular fundus. Images taken during the AI-READI visit are undilated images taken in a dark room.
Optomed Aurora undilated fundus photography
The iCare EIDON is a widefield TrueColor confocal fundus imaging system. AI-READI images using EIDON include two main modalities: Single Field Central IR/FAF and Smart Horizontal Mosaic.
iCare EIDON dilated fundus photography
The Heidelberg Spectralis HRA+OCT is an ophthalmic imaging system that combines optical coherence tomography (OCT) with retinal angiography; imaging is done in a dark room using the following modalities: ONH-RC, PPole-H, and OCTA of the macula.
Heidelberg Spectralis HRA
The DRI OCT Triton is a device from Topcon Healthcare that combines swept-source OCT technology with multimodal fundus imaging. Imaging is done in 12.0X12.0 mm and 6.0X6.0 mm OCTA, and 3D Horizontal and Radial scan modes.
Topcon DRI OCT Triton
The Maestro2 is a robotic OCT and color fundus camera system from Topcon Healthcare. Protocols include 12.0 mm X9.0 mm widefield, 6.0 mm X 6.0 mm 3D macula scan and 6.0 mm X 6.0 mm OCTA.
Topcon Maestro2 3D OCT
Fluorescence Lifetime Imaging Ophthalmoscopy (FLIO) imaging is done by an operator in a dark room and is performed as the last modality of an AI-READI visit.
Heidelberg FLIO
The Zeiss Cirrus 5000 Angioplex is a high-definition optical coherence tomography (OCT) system. Imaging protocols include 512 X 512 and 200 X 200 macula and ONH scans and also OCTA of the macula.
Zeiss Cirrus 5000 Angioplex
The Dexcom G6 is a real-time, integrated continuous glucose monitoring system (iCGM) that directly monitors blood glucose levels without requiring finger sticks; it is used for post-visit data collection for 10 days.
Dexcom G6 continuous glucose monitor
The Garmin Vivosmart 5 Fitness Activity tracker is used to measure data related to physical activity during the 10 days of post-visit data collection.
Garmin Vivosmart 5 fitness tracker
Participants are sent home with an environmental sensor (a custom-designed sensor unit called the LeeLab Anura), which they use for 10 continuous days before returning the devices to the clinical research coordinators for data download.
LeeLab Anura environmental sensor

The dataset gets released as static versions. The Snellen visual acuity variables were dropped in this version of the dataset. The FAIRhub page for version 2.0.0 (2.01 TB, 165,051 files) states that that version of the dataset is no longer accessible. A smaller version is available for pipeline development.
