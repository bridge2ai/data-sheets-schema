Motivation

For what purpose was the dataset created?

  • Response
    The project seeks to create an ethically sourced flagship dataset to enable future research in artificial intelligence and support critical insights into the use of voice as a biomarker of health. The documentation states the goal as facilitating the use of voice as a non-invasive, cost-effective biomarker for the screening, diagnosis, and monitoring of a wide range of health conditions.

Bridge2AI-Voice
Bridge2AI-Voice: An ethically-sourced, diverse voice dataset linked to health information
A collection of data derived from voice recordings with corresponding clinical information. Version 3.1 contains data for 833 participants across five sites in North America, selected based on known conditions which manifest within the voice waveform, including voice disorders, neurological disorders, mood disorders, and respiratory disorders. The release contains data considered low risk: derived audio data features such as spectrograms, but not the original voice recordings, together with phenotypic information acquired during data collection (detailed demographic, clinical, and validated questionnaire data) and metadata related to the specific audio task and recording.

    Why Not Representative
    The data is not representative because it was collected at a limited number of geographic locations.
    Strategies
    Non-probability sample. Patients presenting at specialty clinics and institutions were considered for enrollment and were selected based on membership to five predetermined groups (respiratory disorders, voice disorders, neurological disorders, mood disorders, pediatric); patients were screened for inclusion and exclusion criteria prior to their visit by the project investigators. The documentation describes recruitment across different academic institutions from "high volume expert clinics", outpatient clinics that have developed an expertise in a specific disease area and see more than 50 patients per month from the same disease category. The study metadata in the documentation give a minimum age of 18 years, accept healthy volunteers, and exclude people who do not read or speak English.
    Source Caveats
    The release page lists pediatric among the five predetermined groups; the pediatric cohort is released as a separate dataset (see related_datasets), and the release page calls this one the adult dataset.
  • Description
    The dataset has been audited internally for missingness and consistency by the data release team, and a missingness table is included with the dataset.
    Missing Data Patterns
    Some questions are optional. Each individual answered a common set of questions and then responded to additional questions relevant to their primary diagnostic category.
    Missing Data Causes
    There may be data collection irregularities that caused some information to be missing from individual instances.
  • Missing Data Patterns
    Spectrograms, Mel spectrograms, MFC coefficients, PPGs, and EMAs for sensitive records and audio checks have been removed from v3.1.
    Missing Data Causes
    Removal for sensitive records and audio checks; in addition, some files, whether due to length or other issues, could not generate certain features and so are not included in the bundled data.
  • Missing Data Patterns
    Phenotype data files only contain rows for a participant if at least one column is not missing.
Anomaly Details
Different sites have different collection configurations, and the collection protocol changed over the course of the study.
Confounding factors that might be present in the data: noise artifacts, variations in diagnostic practices, inaccurate questionnaire responses, underreporting.
As participants may have repeated visits, there may be more than one row per participant in the data files, and there is no requirement that the participant provide the same response for each visit; participant information for the same data element may therefore vary across the data file.
Bias Description
Groups that have less trust in the medical system or AI, or that are less proximal to the collection sites, would have been less likely to be recruited.
Participants were screened for different disorders based on site, so they also had their data collected with different devices; distinct iPad devices were used at each site.
Limitation Description
The public data releases do not contain an equal distribution of the disease categories.
Certain aspects of the data (e.g., transcription) were generated using off-the-shelf models that have not been audited for correctness.
Changes in diagnostic criteria or practices could be a source of distribution shift.
  • Warnings
    • The documentation's Healthsheet states that the dataset includes the transcription of free speech tasks; inclusion of offensive or otherwise unsafe content is unlikely but cannot be completely avoided, as research participants are responsible for their choice of language.

    Acquisition Details
    The data was directly observable and reported by subjects. Clinical diagnoses were verified by clinicians reviewing audio and/or imaging data, looking at electronic health records, or medication prescriptions; clinical validation is performed by a qualified physician or practitioner based on established gold standards for diagnosis. The documentation states that the data was collected in the USA and Canada and that five recording sites are included in the dataset.
Mechanism DetailsName
Data collection was conducted using a custom application on a tablet, with a headset used when possible. Voice samples are collected prospectively using a custom software application (the Bridge2AI-Voice App) with the project's protocols; the application was created to collect standardized recordings of acoustic tasks, validated patient questionnaires, and validated patient reported outcomes. The standardized protocol involved the collection of demographic information, health questionnaires, targeted questionnaires inquiring about known confounders for voice, disease specific information, and voice recording tasks such as sustained phonation of a vowel sound. The documentation states that a total of 22 acoustic tasks are recorded through the app. The study protocol names two platforms for collection, the Bridge2AI Voice Web app and the Bridge2AI Voice iOS app.
Bridge2AI-Voice App
Data is collected on iPads (9th or 10th generation) and iPad Air (5th generation) using an Avid AE-36 microphone and an Apple dongle to connect it to the iPad. The data were recorded with a head-mounted headset with a microphone that could be at slightly different distances.

NamePreprocessing DetailsUsed Software
Audio standardization
Raw audio was preprocessed by converting to monaural and resampling to 16 kHz with a Butterworth anti-aliasing filter applied. Derived data were extracted from this standardized audio.
Spectrograms
Time-frequency representations were computed using the short-time Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) with a 25ms window size, 10ms hop length, and a 400-point FFT. Spectrograms were further downsampled by a factor of two in the time domain after derivation.

Articulatory features
Using the Speech Articulatory Coding (sparc) package, the kinematic traces of vocal tract articulators and source features were generated, as well as measures of loudness, periodicity, and pitch. All features were gathered at 50Hz.

    The release page of this dataset notes that the Bridge2AI-Voice Pediatric Dataset is also available on PhysioNet, and the pediatric release page notes the adult dataset in the same way.
