Response
The Bridge2AI-Voice project seeks to create an ethically sourced flagship dataset to enable future research in artificial intelligence and support critical insights into the use of voice as a biomarker of health.
The primary objective stated in the Bridge2AI Voice Data Acquisition protocol is to build a large multi-institutional database of human voices, speech and respiratory sounds that is ethically sourced, diverse, and linked to multimodal health biomarkers to fuel voice AI research.
Establishing a robust multi-institutional dataset that integrates pediatric voice data with demographic information would advance the understanding of voice and disease as well as early detection and intervention.

Response
The availability of large-scale pediatric datasets is limited; privacy issues, consent processes and the need for developmentally appropriate tasks have contributed to the lack of available pediatric data sets that enable machine learning for pediatric voice analysis.
Pediatric and adult voice and speech production are fundamentally different, so evidence and normative data available for adults cannot be generalized to pediatric populations.
The data acquisition protocol notes that the literature is sparser in terms of pediatric voice and speech analysis, partly due to ethical concerns and challenges in data acquisition for this cohort.
  • Strategies
    Patients and healthy volunteers at the Hospital for Sick Children were considered for enrollment. Patients were eligible if they fulfilled the inclusion criteria of 2 to 18 years of age and English proficiency; exclusion criteria included participants over 18 years of age and individuals who were non-verbal. Non-patients, recruited through research postings, were evaluated for eligibility on the same inclusion and exclusion criteria. The Bridge2AI-Voice documentation states that pediatric participants are recruited strictly from the Hospital for Sick Children (SickKids).

Export and format conversion
Data were exported and converted to tab delimited values using an open source library developed by the team. The Bridge2AI-Voice documentation states that pediatric data collected through ReproSchema-UI is extracted and transformed into REDCap format, and subsequently converted to the Brain Imaging Data Structure (BIDS), and that the raw audio files and the questionnaire data retrieved from ReproSchema-UI or exported from REDCap were converted to be compliant with the Brain Imaging Data Structure v1.9.0.
Audio feature derivation and phenotype merging
The code used to preprocess the raw audio waveforms into the Parquet files and to merge the source data into the phenotype files has been made open source in the b2aiprep library. The Parquet feature files comprise spectrograms, Mel spectrograms, Mel-frequency cepstrum coefficients, pitch, estimated electromagnetic articulography, loudness, periodicity and phonetic posteriorgrams, each described with its file.
Version 3.0.0 is the version of b2aiprep cited in the PhysioNet reference list. SenseLab is named in the Bridge2AI-Voice documentation's statement that all processing is performed using b2aiprep and SenseLab; the PhysioNet page names only b2aiprep.
