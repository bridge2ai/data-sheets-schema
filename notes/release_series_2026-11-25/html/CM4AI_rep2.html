Response
To produce ethical, AI-ready datasets of cell architecture, inferred from multimodal data collected for human cell lines, to enable transformative biomedical AI research.
To deliver machine-readable hierarchical maps of cell architecture as AI-Ready data produced from multimodal interrogation of 100 chromatin modifiers and 100 metabolic enzymes involved in cancer, neuropsychiatric, and cardiac disorders in disease-relevant cell lines under perturbed and unperturbed conditions.
To map the spatiotemporal architecture of human cells and use these maps toward the grand challenge of interpretable genotype-phenotype learning.

CM4AI
Cell Maps for Artificial Intelligence (CM4AI)
CM4AI (Cell Maps for Artificial Intelligence) is the Functional Genomics Grand Challenge in the NIH Bridge2AI program. The project is generating multi-modal data including protein-protein interaction (PPI), spatial localization, and genetic perturbation data in MDA-MB-468 breast cancer cells (with or without paclitaxel or vorinostat) and iPSCs (with or without differentiation). Its current release, the June 2026 Data Release (Beta) on the University of Virginia Dataverse, includes perturb-seq data for MDA-MB-468 breast cancer cells with and without treatment and for undifferentiated (parental) KOLF2.1J iPSCs; SEC-MS data for MDA-MB-468 breast cancer cells with and without treatment, undifferentiated KOLF2.1J iPSCs, and iPSC-derived neuron progenitor cells (NPCs), neurons, and cardiomyocytes; AP-MS data for treated MDA-MB-468 breast cancer cells; and IF images in MDA-MB-468 breast cancer cells with and without treatment. Computed cell maps are not included in this release. The project's data-releases page states that CM4AI will deliver machine-readable hierarchical maps of cell architecture as AI-ready data, together with quarterly data releases of map-input data streams, and that CM4AI cell maps are produced from multimodal interrogation of chromatin modifiers, metabolic enzymes, and other proteins involved in cancer, neuropsychiatric, and cardiac disorders. According to the May 2024 preprint, the input data streams are integrated via the multi-scale integrated cell (MuSIC) software pipeline. The data-releases page describes CM4AI as a collaboration of UCSD, UCSF, Stanford, UVA, Yale, UT Austin, UA Birmingham, Simon Fraser University, and the Hastings Center, and reports project figures of 1,374 protein interactions, 53,788 immunofluorescent images, 7,023 total proteins investigated, 11,739 genes targeted, and a data volume of 21.4 TB. The release metadata lists University of California San Diego, University of California San Francisco, Stanford University, and University of Virginia as production locations.

Mechanism DetailsName
Spatial proteomics data are generated using immunofluorescence (IF) subcellular microscopy: IF staining and high-resolution microscopy reveal protein localization and distribution within human cells. File descriptions on the October 2025 release page describe imaging by immunofluorescence-based staining (ICC-IF) and confocal microscopy, with nuclei stained with DAPI (blue channel), endoplasmic reticulum with a calreticulin antibody (yellow channel), microtubules with tubulin antibody (red channel), and an antibody against the protein of interest (green channel).
Immunofluorescence (IF) subcellular microscopy
Protein-protein interaction data are generated using affinity purification mass spectroscopy (AP-MS); the May 2024 preprint describes AP-MS on endogenously tagged cell lines.
Affinity purification mass spectrometry (AP-MS)
Protein-protein interaction data are also generated using size-exclusion chromatography coupled to mass spectrometry (SEC-MS) for proteome-wide complex and interaction mapping, which the May 2024 preprint presents with AP-MS as two orthogonal mass spectrometry-based approaches.
Size-exclusion chromatography mass spectrometry (SEC-MS)
Genetic perturbation data are generated by single-cell CRISPR-Cas perturbation screens by cell type. File descriptions on the October 2025 release page describe an expressed genome-scale CRISPRi Perturbation Cell Atlas in KOLF2.1J human induced pluripotent stem cells mapping transcriptional and fitness phenotypes associated with 11,739 targeted genes.
Single-cell CRISPR perturbation screens (perturb-seq)

Version Details
The project's data-releases page presents the June 2026 Data Release (Beta) as the latest release and lists the May 2024, March 2025, June 2025 and October 2025 Data Releases as its archive. The March 2025 release was described as including perturb-seq data in undifferentiated KOLF2.1J iPSCs; SEC-MS data in undifferentiated KOLF2.1J iPSCs and iPSC-derived NPCs, neurons, and cardiomyocytes; and IF images in MDA-MB-468 breast cancer cells in the presence and absence of chemotherapy (vorinostat and paclitaxel), packaged in RO-Crate format using the FAIRSCAPE framework. The June 2025 release, in its revised version, was described as adding RGB immunofluorescent images, corrections to ro-crate metadata, and changes to naming conventions, and as including SEC-MS data in treated and untreated MDA-MB468 breast cancer cells as well.
