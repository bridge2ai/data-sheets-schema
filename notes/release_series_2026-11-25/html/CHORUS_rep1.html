CHoRUS
CHoRUS Dataset
https://chorus4ai.org/
The CHoRUS dataset is a multicenter, multimodal and high-resolution clinical care dataset produced by the CHoRUS (Patient-Focused Collaborative Hospital Repository Uniting Standards) project, one data generation project of four in the NIH-funded Bridge2AI consortium. The project website describes the CHoRUS project as the collection of multi-modal data across 14 different hospitals. Its "Current Released Dataset" snapshot lists 50,000 patient admissions from ICU, PICU, and NICU, 1.6 Billion rows of EHR OMOP data, 7,642 admissions with radiology data, and 23 Tb of waveform data. Its "Anticipated Final Dataset" snapshot lists 100,000 patient admissions, 9 different data modalities, 14 data contributing hospitals, and 60+ CHoRUS consortium members across 20 different institutions. The NIH project abstract states the project's overarching goal of developing a publicly available, AI-ready critical care dataset, and describes three project pillars: Data, Ethics, and People. As planned content, the abstract names data such as structured electronic health record data, tokenized unstructured electronic health record data, telemetry and EEG waveforms, imaging, and social determinants of health. On ethics, the abstract plans community-facing ethics focus groups to determine what data is appropriate for public sharing, and the project website states that the CHoRUS consortium evaluates community perspectives on clinical care AI to find ways to increase trustworthiness of provenance and privacy in AI.
