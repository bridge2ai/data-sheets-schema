inputs and this job's output files. Explicit grants admit the supported regex
forms. The controller checks executed Bash calls with the same classifier
used for denials, including nonzero exits and ambiguous call/result evidence.

and output identities. Historical attempts keep their original verdicts.

after execution. These controls are not a filesystem sandbox. File-tool paths,
helper arguments, required checks and phase ordering still need independent
review. Lookup permission does not replace ordered chunk reads or immediate
receipt writes. Both final synthetic probes passed all 23 cases on the pinned

source-review v1 and evaluation definitions retain their identities. Both

adaptive thinking with no explicit effort override; native retains default
effort, its observed 200k context and 64k output ceiling per call, and a
10,800-second attempt deadline. These limits do not establish equal realized
effort, deterministic generation or completion cost.

   Receipt coverage and declared judgments alone do not establish entailment.
