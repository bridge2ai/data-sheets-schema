notes: >-
  CM4AI is organized into three pillars (Data, People, and Ethics) across six modules: Data
  Acquisition, Tools, and Standards; Skills and Workforce Development, and Teaming; and
  Ethics. Cell maps are hierarchical directed acyclic graphs in which each node represents an
  assembly of proteins in proximity at a given scale, spanning from large assemblies
  representing cell compartments to small assemblies of proteins in close proximity; the
  project produces them with the Multi-Scale Integrated Cell (MuSIC) pipeline, which embeds
  protein-protein interaction networks with node2vec and immunofluorescence images with a
  Human Protein Atlas deep learning model, integrates the two by contrastive co-embedding,
  detects protein communities at multiple resolutions, and annotates the resulting hierarchy
  against the Gene Ontology and Reactome and with a large language model naming approach.
  Computed cell maps are not included in this release, so those pipeline stages describe
  project tooling and planned future releases rather than processing applied to the data
  released here. The CM4AI project is a collaboration of UCSD, UCSF, Stanford, UVA, Yale, UT
  Austin, UA Birmingham, Simon Fraser University, and the Hastings Center. The University of
  Virginia Dataverse collection carries the notice that it is under review for potential
  modification in compliance with Administration directives.
