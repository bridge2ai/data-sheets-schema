known_biases:
- bias_type: selection_bias
  bias_description: >-
    Groups that have less trust in the medical system or in AI, or that are less proximal to the
    collection sites, would have been less likely to be recruited.
  mitigation_strategy: >-
    The consortium states it hopes to make the data more representative by shifting to remote
    collection and by designing the recruitment approach to control for more variables.
- bias_type: representation_bias
  bias_description: >-
    The sample was collected at a limited number of geographic locations, across five recording
    sites in the United States and Canada, and is limited to fluent English speakers. It is
    therefore not representative of the larger population from which it is drawn.
  mitigation_strategy: >-
    The project documentation states that collection will expand to include Spanish, and that
    remote collection is planned.
- bias_type: measurement_bias
  bias_description: >-
    Participants were screened for different disorders based on site, so cohorts also had their data
    collected on different devices; distinct iPad devices were used at each site. Recordings were
    made with a head-mounted headset whose microphone distance could vary slightly between
    participants, and the clinical diagnosis was made by a single clinician or from an electronic
    health record or prescription depending on the disorder.
  affected_subsets:
  - Disease cohort categories, which are confounded with collection site and device.
- bias_type: annotation_bias
  bias_description: >-
    Each instance carries a single diagnostic label from a single labeler, and diagnostic practices
    vary between sites, so label quality is not uniform across cohorts. The human-level performance
    of the diagnostic task varies widely across the conditions covered.
