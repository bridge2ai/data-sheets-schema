download links are not supplied.

- patient_id: string; unique fictional patient key; no real identifier.

visits.csv would contain 3,600 rows:
- patient_id: string referencing patients.csv patient_id; required.
- visit_ordinal: integer 1, 2, or 3; ordinal only; required.
- systolic_bp_mmhg: numeric systolic blood pressure in millimetres of mercury;
  required; the documentation specifies no observed range or clinical threshold.

outcomes, dates, or geographic information are described.
