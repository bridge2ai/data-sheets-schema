SYNTHETIC CLINICAL METADATA FIXTURE — NOT A REAL DATASET

The Synthetic Outpatient Measurements fixture describes imaginary longitudinal
outpatient tables solely to test documentation software. Its identifier is
https://example.org/synthetic-outpatient-measurements/v1 and the release described
here is version 1, dated 2026-09-13. The example.org identifier is a placeholder,
not a downloadable study or functioning data service.

The hypothetical tables contain 1,200 fabricated patient identifiers and 3,600
fabricated visits, with exactly three visits per fictional patient. They include
age in years, systolic blood pressure in mmHg, and a visit ordinal. No observations
were collected from people or copied from clinical records. No human subjects,
consent process, ethics approval, patient recruitment, or real clinical sites are
involved. The tables themselves are not supplied; this fixture supplies only
documentation for metadata-generation acceptance checks.

The documentation may be shared as a public software test fixture. No separate
data license, contact address, sponsor, or maintenance commitment is declared.
The fixture is not intended for diagnosis, treatment, model training, estimating
population statistics, or assessing the performance of a real clinical dataset.
